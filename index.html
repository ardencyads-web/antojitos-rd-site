Our Story

Dominican Roots,
New York Hustle.

Antojitos RD brings the vibrant energy of Dominican street food to the heart of Staten Island. Every dish is a tribute to the cravings — "antojitos" — that define Dominican culture.

From crispy chimis to loaded yaroas, we cook with the same fire and flavor you'd find on any corner in Santo Domingo. Family recipes, fresh ingredients, no shortcuts — that's the Antojitos way.
